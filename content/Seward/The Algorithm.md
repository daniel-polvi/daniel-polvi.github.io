Just found out about Substack the other day. Totally could post on there and probably make money if I spam essays. Could be worth it. But, I hate algorithms and I refuse to subject anyone to relentless suggestion. I do not want my thoughts to be tainted by the U.S. capitalist disease any more than they already are. 

The amount of strife that algorithms inflict upon me is unreasonable. Recently I have been making an effort to remove any algorithms from my thought patterns mainly to cleanse my mind of consumptive habits and more generally the U.S. Mindset. (Man moves to Alaska to escape society, how original?) 

In a conversation about misogyny the other day I was asked if I was a misogynist. I said yes I am. Woah woah woah. I'm not a misogynist consciously, but having been steeped in misogynist soup my whole life, misogyny is deep inside my world view. This among other recent events reminded me of many of the things I disagree with, but am against my own will seeped in every day.  

I very much disagree with much of what U.S. culture forces us to value in order to survive within it. I have so many specific problems with it I often have to make a conscious effort to be civilized. I have accepted this as just a fact of living in society. We sacrifice certain things so we all get along. But I sometimes find it unbearable. Feeling exploited at every pause and getting bitched because I feel powerless to alter the system. Or just a pervasive feeling that I don't fit in and something is wrong with me, or easier to cope with, something is wrong about the society I live in. Although I cannot change it, I am able to marginalize myself from the culture, this is one reason why I just want to bike around for now. Feel how much I change when I step away for a while. 

Why must everything be exploited and maximized? I think life works better in harmony, and feels better in moderation. Necessary to the American existence is exploitation, and characteristic of the capitalist pathology is maximization.

Modern civilization in America (talking two continents not the United States) was founded on exploitation. The whites of America exploit, and the non-whites are exploited. This has been true ever since the white man step foot on this continent. We exploited the men who already inhabited the land. We exploited the land on which they previously lived in harmony with. We even enslave millions more so we can exploit the land even more. Exploitation is why the earth is falling sick and men are fundamentally mistrustful of each other. It is also the basis of my mother worldview. 

Grappling with how imbedded exploitation is inside of me is difficult. I have no rational personal desire to exploit others, but my intuition is regardless to do so. I know from universal ethics that love, honesty, and reciprocity are of the upmost importance to the rightful continuation of humanity, but regardless my reflex is to fear, lie, and cheat. The opposite of what is rightful is the foundation of the American worldview, my mother worldview. 

Excess. Why must we have so much? Travel light and go far. "Better to need it and not have it than have it and not need it". Money for nothing. Die with zero! Patience to purchase.
