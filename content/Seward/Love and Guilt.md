The past few weeks and peaking the previous few days I have been caught up about two main things. First of all: you can do nothing these days without blood on your hands. Secondarily: Love is the hardest, but most important thing. These two things could fill books on their own, but I'll cram them into one freeform essay, that hopefully isn't too long, because I think they are inherently related. 
​
Anyone who is empathetic and socially aware has certainly come to feel guilty about both particular and general circumstances of life. The world is deeply unjust and both intentionally atrocious and random tragedies are unraveling every day. Examples of all these are endless, but here are my lists of each category of what immediately comes to my mind as a privileged and educated American: 
Injustice: patriarchal domination, unfair legal outcomes based on race and class, unequal economic circumstances based on race or sex, ever increasing surveillance, massive and growing wealth inequality.
