Daniel James Polvi is my name, 

who am I is a really hard question for me to answer. 

In a sentence: I am a man who loves life. 

I grew up and went to school in San Luis Obispo, California. I however was born in Eastern Washington as the youngest of 5 boys. Catholic church was always a part of my childhood, so was engineering and a fascination with science and technology. Above science, I had questions about existence and society, I blame it partially on church, partially on youtube, and partially because we moved around and I gained an outside perspective on society early in life. I went to college and studied economics and philosophy, after leaving mechanical engineering for ideological reasons (didn't want to sell my mind to warmongers).

Philosophy is a huge part me. I love philosophy and try my hardest to live my philosophy, I strongly believe that *philosophia est ars vitae* and philosophy is much of what gives me reason to live and love life.

I am an economist both by education and some divine curse. I chose to study economics because my mind has an inclination for rational economic thought, and I find the insights into humanity and society fascinating. I appreciate how economic thought influences the lives of real people all around the world and I want to understand that so I can do something to meaningfully help people live better.
