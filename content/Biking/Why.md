Why is the question, I don't know is the answer. 

This page, my attempt to know why I want to bike around could end with that one sentence. Three words: I don't know. Maybe there is no real reason, or maybe there are too many reasons I can't comprehend them, much less write them all down. Honestly if I did know why I don't think I would be doing all this. Because why do we keep going is a massive question that many have questioned and far fewer have answered. I don't believe anyone has answered it definitively. 

I already briefly went over this idea of why in [[Crazy Idea]] but that was more material reasoning; a question of why do I want to bike. Currently I am more concerned on why I do anything.
