Travel far and travel wide. 
Travel slow and follow the tide. 
Travel to run and travel to hide. 

Travel won't soothe, but expose the mind
To seek, never to find. 

Travel to think and travel to feel.
Travel to learn and travel to share.
Travel to meet and too soon say goodbye. 

Travel won't change, and neither will you
Only reveal what you've had, but never knew.
