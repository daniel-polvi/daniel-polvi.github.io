In American culture efficiency is the supreme ethical principle that paired with the capitalist pathologies of unlimited wants and gross exploitation of others being justified leads to ironically inefficient outcomes such as human accelerated climate change, human rights violations by corporations and governments worldwide, and an inefficient (unfair) allocation of our limited resources. -> there are many ways to define efficient allocation, I am working under the whatever one it is where everyone on earth is entitled to some minimum standard that they can have an actualized life. <- Our substitution of efficiency as an ethical system have created market conditions that are both fatally unethical and inefficient.
