Figuring out this website, and life. Though I am making an attempt to figure out life, assuming there is anything to be understood, this page only serves as a introduction to this website. 

To figure out this website is something for me to do over time. Right now it is disorganized, unpopulated, and lacking theme, but as I have more to put here It will theoretically turn into something more cohesive. Its intended purpose, as of writing this, is to be a receptacle of life I experience and people I meet and ultimately document during my bike trip from Alaska to Argentina. 

This website is as much for me as it is for you. For me it is something for me to look back on when I am old and boring and to be like, 'oh yeah I remember back when I was young and biked a million miles'. Having this be public has potential external benefits so I will make it open. I hope you will be entertained and inspired, but of course feel free to be disgusted, disappointed, or concerned for my well being. Genuinely, if you feel anyway different than baseline due to the consumption of this media, my work is successful.
