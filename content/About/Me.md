Daniel James Polvi is my name, daniel-polvi.github.io is my domain.

Who am I is a really hard question for me to answer. 

In a sentence: I am a man who loves life. 

I went to college and studied economics and philosophy, after leaving mechanical engineering for ideological reasons. I grew up in California, on a nice part of the coast. I was born in Eastern Washington as the youngest of 5 boys. Catholic church was always a part of my childhood, so was engineering and a fascination with science and technology. Above science though I had questions about existence and society, I blame it partially on church and partially because we moved around and I developed an outside perspective on my own life quite early. Philosophy is a big part of my life as well. I love philosophy and try my hardest to live my philosophy, I do strongly believe that *philosophia est ars vitae* and philosophy is much of what gives me drive to live life artistically. 

On that note, am I an artist? I guess I am an artist in the sentiment expressed above, I see my life as a work of art. Wow that's egotistical.
