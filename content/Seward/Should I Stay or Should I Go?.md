I did a little bit of math and realized I can have enough money to just leave Alaska in December, travel and live in Latin America for 4-5 months and have just enough money to bike the thing. The idea would be to spend 5 weeks with Mutale in Mexico then Brasil, and then by myself elsewhere in South America. 

I am not super happy living in Alaska. I do like the natural beauty, but I'm just a little bit bored, lonely, and feel as though I am not living in alignment with myself and I want to change. The interesting thing too I realized today is that I am allowed to do this if I want to. Like it is my life and I can spend it how I want to. Shout out Kevin Houser for the existential knowledge. 

It is just definitely a big risk though. There are many risk, but the main risk I'm concerned about in this instance is just not wanting to come back, or somehow jeopardizing my ability to bike. The main thing would be money, not having enough of it. Money is always an object whether you like it or not. I personally really dislike money. I hate to spend it, and I hate even more I have to spend so much of my life to make it. Regardless of how I feel, money makes the world go round and I need it to achieve my goals; or so I think. I have been trying to imagine ways to make money without a full time, in person job, or just live a life where I spend no money. 

I have lived in foreign countries without money before, and it's been the best times of my life. But at that time I was still in school and my parents were still supporting me when I was at home. Now it is different. Now I don't really have my own home to come back to, and no real responsibility to collect upon. I have this constructed goal of biking the Pan-American, but to a significant degree this plan is just to give myself a goal. There is no real reason for me to do it besides it's dope and it gives me an immediate goal to orient myself towards. On second thought the idea that I have no backup plan is false, I can always bail out back to Boise. I just don't really want to. I want to make it on my own, its a character flaw of mine. 

Money is the thing though. Idk how to balance the line between being broke and not caring and having like 1000 bucks so I can bail or go to the hospital ya know. My plan in life so far is become homeless, but I still am not past the idea that I can live without money or a job. So two questions remain, coincidentally both named after great rock songs: Money, and Should I Stay Or Should I Go. 

As of right now 11:35pm on Wednesday September 2nd, I am leaning towards going. But to be fair I am in a bit of a state right now and shouldn't be making life altering decisions. Or should I...

Update from a few days later, yeah I'm going to go to Brazil. If my jobs don't want to give me 5 weeks off ill just quit and stay down there for longer, but I will probably come back as I want to experience the winter. 

Okay another update from a week later. That was a phase, I think unhappiness coming and going rapidly is part of who I am; I will feel unhappy anywhere. I want to experience the winter, plus I got the time off so I'll just come back after Brazil. Super excited to go! I think this mindset I have of: Unhappy, therefore run far away is unproductive. My problems are mostly internal and those will follow me anywhere, it's more a matter of if your environment is distracting you from them. In Alaska the environment is psychedelic (literally: to reveal the psyche). You are confronted with a deeper part of your being and there is not much to distract you from them. I must see this as a blessed opportunity for introspection however hard it is.
