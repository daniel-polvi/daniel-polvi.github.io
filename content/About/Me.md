Daniel James Polvi is my name, 

Who I am is not for me to know.

Philosophy is an important part of my life. I love philosophy and try to live philosophy. 

I am an economist both by education and divine curse. I studied economics and philosophy in college. I have an inclination for economic thought and I find the insights into humanity and society fascinating. 

I am a traveler. I try not to be over romantic about traveling, but I am. I feel actualized when I am out of place. I prefer to travel by bike, but road trips, hitchhiking, trains, or busses all scratch the itch, airplanes are a necessary evil. My urge to travel stems from an omnipresent feeling of strangeness. I have no delusion that traveling will make me a better person or cure this feeling of strangeness I have but I do find it fun and want to do as much of it as I can. 

I find it incredibly painful to write about myself so I am going to stop now.
