This website is a collection of documents about life through the perspective of [[me]]. 

I am going to travel the world and write about it as I go. I started this website after I graduated university and started planning a PanAmerican bike ride (see [[Biking The Americas/]] for more). Who knows where life will take me after that, but it is my indefinite goal to travel the world in order to understand the variety of existence.

This website is not intended to be polished and or cohesive. I want a collection of my experiences and thoughts and I see more good than harm in letting anyone see it.
