The next section will be Vancouver to San Diego, the North South length of the contiguous  United States. 

From Vancouver to Seattle should be nice I assume there will be some nice bike paths there and be a cyclist friendly area. Starting in Seattle there is an established biking route called the Great American Wheel Route (GARW), going all the way to D.C.. I can take this path for a few hundred miles to over by the Hanford site in Washington, something I want to see. The route goes right past here and I would break off and go down into Richland, where I was born. Honestly I don't expect much out of Richland I more just want to go to my old home and feel nostalgic. I lived there for only 7 years, so I only have those dreamy early childhood memories that walk the line between reality and childish delusion, but I think it'll be strange and somehow deeply emotional to find myself back there. 

From Richland I want to go to Boise to see my parents and chimp out in a home. I don't expect Boise to be an amazing city, but I would like to see my parents and my brother for a bit while I still can. Leaving here is where a dilemma emerges. A fork in my road. I don't need to decide yet, obviously. Me being there is roughly going to happen in 1 year. Regardless I can't help but obsess over maps and dream about where I want to see. 

There are infinite options for where I could bike to, which, while magical, also makes decisions hard. The end goal of this section is San Diego, Where again I can see Mutale and hope she still wants to bike Latin America with me. 

I have come up with 3 loose options. 

1. Boise - Yellowstone - Tetons - Denver - New Mexico desert - San Diego
2. ​Boise - Vegas - Slab City - San Diego 
3. Boise - Tahoe - 395 - Slab City - San Diego 

1 is the longest by far, probably around 2600-3000 miles, but also brings me places I've never been to the problem would be winter and cold Mountains. I suppose this section would also take 2 ish months. Bringing me to San Diego around December or January. 

2 is significantly shorter maybe like 1100 miles. Would only take about a month... 

Okay I stepped away from this mid sentence and by the next day after talking with Mr. Xaiver Higginbotham, decided to just do the longer one. The bike life isn't about rushing from point to point. Frankly life by any means is not about rushing from point to point. There is also a potentially that we bike together to Florida thru the south. It seems interesting to see Texas and other parts of the south. The weather may be total dog shit when we are doing it, but that is arguably part of the 'fun'. 

I honestly find writing about all this planning stuff quite boring, and I expect that anyone reading this will also find it quite boring. However I think it has "archival value" for lack of a better word. By recording now what I think I want to do I will also be able to have a reference for how my plans change with my experience on the road. 

Furthermore with all these notes and essays I don't mean to publish some continuous finished project, just a large mass of ideas that I could later turn into something coherent. I think once I actually ride the sections the pages of planning will be either created into massive pages of the whole section, both planning and experience. Or become parts of folders. Idk yet and thats okay.
