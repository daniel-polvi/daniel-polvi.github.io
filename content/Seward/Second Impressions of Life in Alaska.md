​What ever happened to just doing shit because you want to, because its cool and romantic to you? Something I appreciate about the Alaskan way of life is the high value of freedom and the delicate balance between romance and pragmatism. It is not easy to end up here by mistake, and you need to be a certain kind of person to enjoy living here. In many ways nature still rules and does not give a fuck about your existence, and frankly it's a rough place. There is a certain romance to this type of life, but it also requires a high degree of pragmatism to not get fucked. In the less than two months I've been here I have seen first hand the struggles people face when trying to cope with life here. Alcoholism and drug addiction is huge here, suicide rates are abnormally high, and just a sometimes harsh and salty outlook on life. 

I don't know how to feel about this outlook. The distrust many have is fast to wear down a loving heart. However, I see why the feeling is prevalent; to thrive here you need intentions. Intensions are often miss-matched and can destructively interfere.
